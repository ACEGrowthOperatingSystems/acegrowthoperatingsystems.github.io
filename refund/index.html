ACE Growth Operating Systems is operated by LifeGuide Group LLC. This policy explains when refunds are available.

5. How to request a refund. Email AceGrowth.os@gmail.com from the address on your account, with your name and order details. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days.

6. Changes to this policy. We will tell you about material changes by email and by a notice on our website.

7. Contact. AceGrowth.os@gmail.com
