Privacy Policy

Effective 4 September 2026

This policy explains what this website collects and what happens to it. It describes what the site actually does today, not what a template says a website usually does. If the site changes, this page changes with it.

ACE Growth Operating Systems is operated by LifeGuide LLC. Where this policy says “we”, it means that company.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form and submit. That is:

  • Your first name
  • Your email address
  • The business problem you selected, if you used the diagnostic

Nothing else is collected. There is no account, no login, and no payment on this site.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising or tracking cookies
  • No analytics or visitor tracking
  • No location data
  • No profiles built about you across other websites
If we later add analytics or advertising measurement, this section will be updated before those tools go live, not afterwards.

Why we collect it

To send you what you asked for, and to follow up about ACE Growth Operating Systems. That is the only reason. We do not sell, rent or trade your information to anyone.

Who else handles it

Your submission passes through service providers we use to run the business. They process it on our instructions and for no purpose of their own:

  • GitHub Pages — serves this website
  • n8n — receives form submissions and routes them to us
  • Email and CRM providers — store your details so we can reply

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, or until it is no longer needed for the purpose you gave it to us for.

Your choices

Email us and we will action any of these:

  • See what we hold about you
  • Correct anything wrong
  • Delete it entirely
  • Stop receiving email from us

Every email we send also carries an unsubscribe link.

Children

This site is for business use and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Submissions travel over an encrypted connection and are held in access-controlled accounts. No system is perfectly secure, and we would rather say that plainly than claim otherwise.

Changes
