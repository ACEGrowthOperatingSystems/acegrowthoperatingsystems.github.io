The friction

Campaign work should not disappear between tools and teams.

ACE-MKT creates a traceable path from a qualified need to the assets, owners, destinations, and follow-up it requires.

Unclear intake

Requests arrive without the context needed to qualify or prioritize them.

Disconnected production

Messages and assets multiply without reliable source, status, or destination bindings.

Weak handoffs

Owners, consent controls, and follow-up expectations are easy to lose at execution time.

Six-stage experience

Every stage has a purpose, owner, and receipt.

  • Intake: capture the need and the operating context.
  • Qualification: confirm fit before production begins.
  • Plan: bind the message, audience, channel, and intended outcome.
  • Asset package: create traceable derivatives from approved source material.
  • Fulfillment handoff: assign the owner, destination, and release condition.
  • Follow-up: preserve the next action and learning loop.
